# ColorBlast Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 20, 2026  
**Developer:** FlyHighInc ("we", "us")  
**Contact:** lilchunga@gmail.com  
**App:** ColorBlast for iPhone and Android

## Summary

- ColorBlast has no accounts, sign-in, chat, or in-app purchases.
- Your game progress stays on your device. We do not receive it.
- ColorBlast uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect device information to provide ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.
- In the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Google's consent system manages applicable advertising consent choices.
- You can review or change your privacy choices in the app's Settings where this option is available.

## 1. Information Stored on Your Device

ColorBlast stores the following information locally:

- Current game progress, high scores, and game statistics.
- Sound, haptic feedback, and gameplay preferences.
- Tutorial completion status.
- Advertising frequency information used to limit how often ads appear.

This information is not transmitted to our servers. We do not operate accounts or cloud storage for game progress.

Deleting ColorBlast normally removes this locally stored information, subject to any device backup or restoration functionality.

## 2. Advertising and Data Collection

ColorBlast uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to display:

- Optional rewarded advertisements that allow you to continue a game.
- Occasional full-screen advertisements between games.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

- IP addresses, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
- Device identifiers, depending on the operating system and applicable permissions.
- Information about advertisements viewed or interacted with.
- App interactions and performance information.
- Diagnostic information, including crash reports.

This information may be used for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

ColorBlast does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

For further information, see:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

**EEA, UK, and Switzerland:** ColorBlast uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required.

Depending on your choices and applicable law, you may receive non-personalized or limited advertisements, or no advertisements.

**Changing your choices:** Where required, you can access Privacy choices in the app's Settings.

**iPhone:** ColorBlast does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and is configured to request non-personalized advertisements.

**Android:** You can manage your advertising identifier through your device's privacy or Google settings.

Your consent preferences are managed on your device through Google's consent system.

## 4. Offline Use

ColorBlast can be played without an internet connection.

When offline, advertising requests are not made. Game progress and settings remain stored locally.

## 5. Children's Privacy

ColorBlast is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you have concerns about a child's information in connection with ColorBlast, contact us at lilchunga@gmail.com.

## 6. Data Security and Retention

Google's advertising SDK transmits information using encrypted connections.

Google determines the retention periods for information it processes according to its policies.

Locally stored game information generally remains on your device until you delete the app or its data.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information, including access, deletion, correction, and objection to certain processing.

You can manage advertising consent where available, adjust device advertising settings, or delete ColorBlast.

For information managed by Google, visit [Google's Data & Privacy controls](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy).
